The Ecosystemic Flâneur

The Flâneur

Wandering: a state of being in the world.

Wandering is going out into the world without knowing in advance what you will find there, with a value, with a question, and with an attention that guides your steps. The ecosystemic flâneur is not an onlooker from the side like the classic flâneur: they are part of a living web of people and places, shaping it and shaped by it, wandering alone or with others, and returning with a new understanding.

01 · A journey between worlds

The method was not born all at once

It grew over years, out of a curiosity that led from place to place, from idea to idea and from person to person. Worlds that seemed far apart met, talked, were tested in the field, and slowly became one way of looking, learning and working with people and places.

You can choose any point in the web and follow the threads that connect it to the others.Visited: 1 of 10

Starting point · The city

The flâneur

It all began with walking. The urban flâneur moves through the city with no set destination: observing, noticing, getting lost, meeting people and places, and discovering possibilities out of the movement and the attention themselves. This figure was the starting point of the whole way.

Meet the Flâneur

An early root

“The Urban Flâneur as a Tool for Urban Research”

A master's thesis asked whether wandering, observation and movement in urban space could be a way of producing knowledge. Its central concept is the “discerning approach”: a gaze that discerns what the systematic gaze misses. This is one of the roots of the way, not its frame.

The academic background

Play · A view from outside

The court jester

The ability to question conventions, to disrupt the familiar view, to bring in play, and to reveal what is hidden in plain sight. The court jester looks at the world from outside its usual frames, and from there sees what others have stopped seeing.

Points of view

Edward de Bono and the Six Thinking Hats

An encounter with different ways of thinking, and with the idea that you can deliberately change the way you approach a question or a situation. The same question looks different from each hat, and the same street looks different from each gaze.

Hosting · Greece

Art of Hosting

Hosting meaningful conversations, collective wisdom, shared learning and participatory processes. The learning also passed through the table: hosting practices and shared meals, in which welcoming guests, the encounter and the shared experience were themselves a way to learn.

Listening · Germany

Theory U

Sensing, listening, presence and emerging possibilities. The way met this approach up close: slowing down, listening also to what has not yet been said, and letting the new appear before rushing to solve.

A shared dream · Berlin

Dragon Dreaming

In Berlin: shared dreaming, planning and participation. How one person's dream becomes the dream of a group, and how a shared dream turns into action in the world.

People, communities and places · Europe

European placemaking

A placemaking conference in Valencia, where wandering also met the World Café, and belonging to the European placemaking network. Encounters and exchanges with professionals from different countries opened other ways of thinking about the relations between people, communities and places, and broadened both the understanding and the work itself.

In the field · Israel and Europe

Dozens of workshops of getting lost

Dozens of workshops, explorations and experiments in getting lost in space. They were called “An invitation to get lost”, under the name “The Flâneur – Tours of Wonder”. Here it became clear that the method does not grow only out of reading and thinking, but out of a repeated encounter with real places and people.

What grew out of all these

The Ecosystemic Flâneur

What matters is not that the flâneur met many methods. They explored them, practised them, interpreted them through their own work, and slowly found their own way to connect them. The Ecosystemic Flâneur is not a combination of existing methods. It is an original approach that keeps developing within the work itself.

To the method

The one who walked the way

The Flâneur

The creator and developer of the Ecosystemic Flâneur. A community artist, researcher, facilitator and designer of participatory processes, and founder of Osim Makom | Community Placemaking. They work at the crossroads of art, community, place and urban life.

Instead of creating for a community, they design the space, the process and the encounter that allow the community itself to be the creator.

This is also the heart of the method. The flâneur does not bring a ready answer to the place. They create conditions in which what already exists can be seen, and what can still grow can be discovered together.

02 · From the journey to the method

Out of the journey, seven movements

Over the years the same movement returned again and again, in every place and with every group. Seven movements took shape out of it. They are living movements, not a procedure: they change in form and length according to the context, and from celebration you return to value with an understanding that has changed.

Value → Letting go → Wandering → Attunement → Discernment → Creation → Celebration

The method in brief

A dialogue with the surroundings

Personal wandering

An exploration of the urban ecosystem: places, landscapes, stories, traces, connections and unexpected discoveries. Knowledge grows out of an ongoing conversation between the person and the surroundings. You go out, notice, discern, and come back with something you did not know you were looking for.

A dialogue with people

Participatory wandering

An exploration of the community ecosystem: residents, local knowledge, memories, points of view, experiences and shared wisdom. The answer is not set in advance by the facilitator. It develops out of collective intelligence. A facilitated process for a community, a place, an organisation or a school, not necessarily a walk, and sometimes over several meetings with a shared harvest between them.

The same seven movements on both paths, expressed differently. You choose the path at the start of the journey. It does not follow from the participants' identity or from the size of the group.

The two paths are neither separate nor contradictory. They are two complementary ways of exploring the world, and they lead into each other: personal wandering can reveal a question that leads to a shared inquiry with residents, and a shared inquiry can change the way we see and explore the space itself. New knowledge grows out of movement and dialogue between people, places and their surroundings, and from it initiatives, community action and change can grow.

The mechanism that accompanies the experience

Digital tools to go wandering

The adaptive mechanism puts together a journey according to the level of wandering, the mode and the place you tell it about. Beside it is a single personal journey, in five stages:

  1. Entry
  2. Movement
  3. Discernment
  4. Trace
  5. Return

The five stages are the structure of one outing in the mechanism, and they are not the seven movements. Nothing is sent: what you write stays in your browser.

The view beneath the method

Five insights for life

Insights that grew out of the way, not commandments. Directions for attention, judgement and action in a living, connected world. They nourish the wandering, but the method itself is the seven movements.

The middle measure

Recognising the right measure, without hoarding, consuming or taking more than is needed. Not mediocrity and not automatic compromise, but judgement in every situation.

The wisdom of many

No one sees the whole picture. When different people share what they know, an understanding can grow that none of them would have reached alone.

Diversity is life

A diversity of people, species, points of view and ways of knowing gives a living system richness, adaptability and possibilities.

Listening is wisdom

To listen is to acknowledge that our point of view is partial. That is how we learn what cannot be seen from a single point of view.

Appreciating what is

Before looking for more, noticing what is already here and appreciating it: people, connections, places and opportunities.

03 · Where the method can lead

Entering the method, together

Each meeting is built around the place, the people and the question that concerns you. A few directions you can set out in:

Workshops and guided wanderings

A guided outing into a place, for one person, a group or a team. In the city, in the neighbourhood or in nature.

Lectures and seminars

An encounter with the idea: from EGO to ECO, machine and living being, and the flâneur as a way of reading a place as a living system. Also as a series of meetings.

Professional training

For facilitators, educators and professionals who want to lead wandering themselves.

Participatory and community processes

A process over several meetings around the place, the story, the strengths, the challenges and the goal, with a shared harvest between them.

Organisations and educational institutions

Wandering as a way to see an organisation or a school as a living system: listening, bringing up distributed knowledge, and learning together from the space.

Research and collaborations

Courses, research, methodological development and applied projects with institutions and partners from different fields.
